Q1 2024 Product Analytics Case Study

Mobile Game Funnel & Monetization Analysis

Analyzing user progression, retention bottlenecks, and monetization opportunities across 50,000 acquired users.

Dashboard

Executive Summary

During Q1 2024, the game acquired 50,000 new users, providing an opportunity to evaluate how effectively players progressed from acquisition through activation, engagement, and monetization.

While 81.4% of installers opened the application, only 49.5% completed signup and 27.3% reached level completion. Monetization analysis further revealed that only 11.2% of acquired users ultimately reached the rewarded-ad claim stage.

Project Overview

As part of the Q1 2024 product performance review, this analysis evaluates how newly acquired users progressed through the mobile game's lifecycle, from installation and onboarding to engagement and monetization.

Using behavioral event data from 50,000 users, the project identifies retention bottlenecks, evaluates monetization effectiveness, and uncovers opportunities to improve player experience and business performance.
