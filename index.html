Mobile Game Funnel & Monetization Analysis

Q1 2024 Product Analytics Case Study

Interactive Dashboard

Executive Summary

During Q1 2024, the game acquired 50,000 new users. This analysis evaluates user progression through acquisition, activation, engagement, and monetization funnels to identify retention bottlenecks and revenue opportunities.

Key Insights

  • 81.4% of users opened the app after installation.
  • Only 27.3% reached level completion.
  • Only 11.2% reached the rewarded-ad claim stage.
  • Organic acquisition generated 40% of level completions.
  • iOS users showed stronger monetization behavior.
